# 1. Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the MarginFlow platform.

It defines the purpose of the product, its intended users, operational scope, core philosophy and the principles that guide its design and future development.

This document serves as the primary entry point for anyone seeking to understand the MarginFlow platform before reading the remaining project documentation.

---

# 2. Product Vision

MarginFlow is a hospitality operations platform that centralises business-critical operational and financial information into a single integrated system.

The platform is designed to replace fragmented spreadsheets, disconnected software and manual administrative processes with structured, reliable and auditable workflows.

MarginFlow enables hospitality businesses to make operational decisions based on accurate data rather than assumptions.

---

# 3. Mission

The mission of MarginFlow is to simplify hospitality management by providing a single platform capable of managing operational, financial and analytical processes across the entire business.

The platform aims to reduce administrative workload, improve data accuracy and provide management with meaningful operational insight.

---

# 4. What MarginFlow Is Not

MarginFlow is not:

* an accounting system;
* a payroll system;
* a Point of Sale (POS) system;
* a generic ERP intended for every industry.

Instead, MarginFlow focuses exclusively on operational management for hospitality businesses while integrating with specialist platforms where appropriate.

---

# 5. Target Market

MarginFlow is designed for organisations operating within the hospitality industry.

Examples include:

* Restaurants
* Cafés
* Bars
* Hotels
* Gastropubs
* Event Venues
* Multi-site Hospitality Groups

---

# 6. Primary Users

The platform is intended for users responsible for managing hospitality operations.

Typical users include:

* Business Owners
* Directors
* Operations Managers
* General Managers
* Executive Chefs
* Head Chefs
* Kitchen Managers
* Finance Teams
* Purchasing Managers

Different users may have different permissions depending on their organisational role.

---

# 7. Core Business Areas

MarginFlow is organised around five interconnected operational areas:

* Procurement
* Production
* Inventory
* Commercial Performance
* Operational Intelligence

Every module contributes to one or more of these operational areas.

---

# 8. Core Objectives

MarginFlow has several strategic objectives.

## Centralisation

Provide a single source of operational and financial information.

## Accuracy

Ensure calculations are reliable, auditable and consistent across all modules.

## Automation

Reduce repetitive manual work through structured workflows and AI-assisted processes.

## Visibility

Provide clear insight into business performance using real-time operational data.

## Scalability

Support businesses ranging from single-site operations to enterprise multi-site organisations.

---

# 9. Functional Scope

MarginFlow is organised into a number of integrated operational modules.

These include, but are not limited to:

* Dashboard
* Sales
* Suppliers
* Products
* Invoice Management
* AI Invoice Processing
* Recipe Costing
* Menu Engineering
* Stock Management
* Labour Analysis
* Reporting
* Business Configuration

Each module operates independently while contributing to a shared operational dataset and a single source of truth.

---

# 10. Core Principles

The platform is developed according to the following principles.

### Simplicity

Complex operational processes should be presented through simple and intuitive interfaces.

### Reliability

Operational and financial data must always be trustworthy.

### Transparency

Users should understand how calculations are performed and how decisions are reached.

### Flexibility

The platform should support different business models through configuration rather than custom development.

### Consistency

Similar actions should behave consistently throughout the application.

---

# 11. Platform Philosophy

Every capability within MarginFlow should:

* solve a genuine operational problem;
* minimise manual administration;
* maintain a single source of truth;
* avoid duplicated information;
* support both single-site and multi-site organisations;
* remain understandable by non-technical hospitality users.

Complexity should exist in the business logic, never in the user experience.

---

# 12. Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is designed to assist operational users rather than replace them.

Its purpose is to:

* reduce manual data entry;
* identify operational patterns;
* automate repetitive operational tasks;
* improve efficiency;
* support informed decision-making.

AI may recommend, classify, extract and automate information where appropriate.

Users remain responsible for approving operational and financial decisions that affect the business.

---

# 13. Long-Term Vision

MarginFlow is intended to become the central operating platform for hospitality businesses.

Future development will continue expanding operational capabilities while maintaining:

* a unified user experience;
* consistent business rules;
* a shared operational data model;
* scalable architecture;
* seamless integrations with specialist platforms.

The long-term objective is to provide a platform where operational, financial and analytical information coexist within a single ecosystem.

---

# 14. Success Criteria

MarginFlow succeeds when hospitality businesses can:

* operate from one integrated platform instead of multiple disconnected systems;
* trust every operational metric;
* reduce manual administration;
* make decisions based on reliable data;
* scale operations without increasing operational complexity.
