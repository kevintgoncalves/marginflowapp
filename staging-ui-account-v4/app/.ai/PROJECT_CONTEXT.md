# 1. Purpose

This document provides the strategic context of the MarginFlow project.

It explains why the platform exists, the problems it solves, the principles behind its design and how every part of the application contributes to a single operational ecosystem.

Every AI assistant, developer and contributor should understand this document before working on the project.

---

# 2. Why MarginFlow Exists

MarginFlow was created from real operational experience within the hospitality industry.

The project was not designed from theory or market research alone.

It was built to solve the daily operational challenges faced by hospitality managers responsible for purchasing, food cost, labour, suppliers, stock control and financial performance.

Traditional hospitality management often relies on multiple disconnected systems and spreadsheets. Information becomes duplicated, inconsistent and difficult to trust.

MarginFlow exists to eliminate this fragmentation.

---

# 3. The Problem

Most hospitality businesses manage critical operational data across several independent systems.

Typical examples include:

* supplier invoices stored separately from purchasing records;
* recipes maintained in spreadsheets;
* stocktakes disconnected from purchasing;
* labour analysed independently from sales;
* gross profit calculated manually;
* supplier pricing difficult to compare over time;
* operational decisions based on incomplete information.

Although each individual process may work, the business lacks a single reliable operational picture.

---

# 4. The MarginFlow Approach

MarginFlow is built around one central idea:

> Every operational process should contribute to a single, reliable operational dataset.

Instead of treating invoices, products, recipes, stock, labour and sales as separate systems, MarginFlow connects them into one continuous operational workflow.

Every module either creates, enriches or consumes operational data.

---

# 5. Data Before Features

MarginFlow is not organised around pages.

It is organised around data.

For example:

* invoices create purchasing data;
* suppliers provide pricing information;
* products become the foundation of recipes;
* recipes determine theoretical food cost;
* sales measure commercial performance;
* stock validates operational accuracy;
* labour provides productivity analysis;
* reporting combines information from every operational area.

Each module strengthens the value of every other module.

---

# 6. Hospitality First

MarginFlow is designed exclusively for hospitality businesses.

Every workflow should reflect how restaurants, cafés, pubs, hotels and hospitality groups actually operate.

The objective is never to force businesses to adapt to software.

The software should adapt to operational reality.

---

# 7. AI as an Operational Assistant

Artificial Intelligence exists to remove repetitive administration.

AI assists by:

* reading invoices;
* extracting structured information;
* recognising suppliers;
* suggesting product matches;
* identifying inconsistencies;
* reducing manual work.

AI does not replace operational judgement.

Users always remain responsible for reviewing and approving business-critical information.

---

# 8. User Experience Philosophy

Hospitality professionals are busy.

The application should minimise clicks, reduce repetitive tasks and present information clearly.

Users should feel confident using the platform without requiring technical knowledge.

Operational complexity should remain hidden behind intuitive workflows.

---

# 9. Long-Term Product Direction

MarginFlow is intended to become the operational platform that hospitality businesses open first every day.

The platform should gradually replace disconnected spreadsheets and isolated operational tools while integrating with specialist external systems where appropriate.

Growth should always strengthen the existing ecosystem rather than adding isolated features.

---

# 10. The Role of Documentation

Documentation is a core component of the project.

It defines how MarginFlow should behave.

Implementation follows documentation.

Whenever uncertainty exists, documentation represents the authoritative reference for future development.

---

# 11. The Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is expected to become a permanent development partner of the project.

AI should:

* understand the business domain;
* understand the platform architecture;
* follow the documented principles;
* propose improvements where appropriate;
* never invent undocumented functionality;
* always distinguish between existing functionality and future proposals.

The quality of AI assistance depends directly on the quality of the documentation.

---

# 12. Success Definition

MarginFlow succeeds when hospitality businesses can manage purchasing, production, inventory, labour and commercial performance from one trusted operational platform.

Success is measured by:

* reduced administration;
* increased operational visibility;
* improved profitability;
* better business decisions;
* higher confidence in operational data.
